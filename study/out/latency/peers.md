Generated by `python3 research/peers` then `python3 research/peers/report.py`.

## Answers

**Followers exist but are small.** 29 non-latency wallets buy the side the bots just bought, a median 2-5 s later, far more often than chance (direction agreement >= 0.65, z >= 4); together they made +10,323 taker PnL on $417,399. In 5m every non-latency taker lag bucket loses; fills 1-30 s after a burst lose 0.9-2.2% per USD, so copying the bots after the fact does not pay.

What tracks profit everywhere is the edge of first-block entries (rho_edge0_roi +0.31 to +0.86), i.e. signal and sizing, not raw latency.

**Being late costs price, and in 5m half the edge.** In 5m, burst entries in the first block earn +3.07% per USD at 0.83 c above the first print; entries 1-5 s later earn +1.48% at 3.27 c above it. Bursts are crowded (5m: median 4 latency wallets per burst, 11 USD-weighted), and the big bots land in the first block in 35-65% of contested bursts: nobody owns the move.

**One operator, many wallets:** timing cannot separate siblings from rival bots that react to the same tick, so groups are called on per-market net position and PnL moving together (corr >= 0.7 for both; rival bots sit at 0.0-0.3). The two top 5m bots are rivals, not siblings (per-market position corr 0.14, PnL corr 0.14 over 571 markets). The near-identical trio 0x576b0696fd5a9225d66fd9500fd98f5be10b0cab, 0xeebde7a0e019a63e6b476eb425505b7b3e6eba30, 0x2ed94d1a460379292d2e3b46f2cf7d94b033f271 is NOT one book either (position corr -0.14 to +0.31 in 5m/15m/hourly): same software, separate accounts.

**Caveats:**

- Lags are Polygon block timestamps (about 2 s blocks); sub-block ordering is invisible, so ties are real ties at this resolution only.
- A follower here may be a slower spot reactor rather than a copier: both trade the bot side 2-5 s later. Their 0.05-0.41 lag-0 co_move share suggests many react to spot themselves.
- Operator groups are union-find over pairs, so chains can join wallets that never correlate directly; q_corr_min / pnl_corr_min show the weakest edge inside each group.
- Burst analysis uses taker fills only. The 15m trio is mostly maker by fill count, so its speed rank describes only its taker side.
